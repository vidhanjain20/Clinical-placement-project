# Clinical Learning Navigator

**Don't just log your placement. Navigate it.**

A personalised placement companion for medical students that turns real clinical experiences into a dynamic map of what they know, what they're missing, and what to do next.

## Status: Pre-build — design complete, MVP scoped

## The one-line problem

Medical students finish placements having seen many patients but still have significant gaps — because clinical exposure is opportunistic, not planned. Existing tools either record what you've done or help you study content. None connect the two around the individual learner.

## The product

Student speaks or types what happened on placement → AI extracts conditions, skills, investigations, gaps → system compares against their accumulating history → returns 1–3 concrete priorities: what to seek, practise, or learn next.

The differentiator is the trajectory. A single-note diary is easy to copy. "You've now flagged ECG interpretation as a weakness three times and haven't yet encountered syncope" — that requires a longitudinal model, not a log.
